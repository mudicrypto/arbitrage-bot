# Risk policy

M1–M5 support simulation and paper trading only. M5 provides forward paper execution using public data and simulated funds; no live execution engine exists. Live mode is absent from Rust types, rejected by the API and prohibited by a database constraint. No private keys, signed exchange requests, order endpoints or live transports exist. Do not request real API credentials or submit real orders.

No leverage, margin borrowing or futures. USD 5,000 is a research assumption, not an authorized deposit or trading budget. Public feeds, simulated execution and any future live transport require separate capabilities and permissions.

Before any execution implementation: independent pre-trade checks, per-asset/total position and notional limits, cash reservations, price/quantity filter checks, stale/gapped feed rejection, kill switch, rate limits, durable intent before submission, unknown-outcome reconciliation, cancellation-race handling and restart recovery must be implemented and tested. Never resubmit unknown outcomes blindly.

Never infer a fill from a touched price. Model queue uncertainty, latency, partial fills, maker/taker fees and actual fee assets. Preserve deterministic replay. Performance reports must include adverse selection, slippage, inventory risk, fee-asset conversion and operating costs. No profitability claim can be made from M1.

Local API has no authentication and must remain loopback-only. Compose password is a documented local default. Production security, backup, retention, auth and operational runbooks remain future work.

M2 permits live public market-data collection only. It provides no private authentication, order submission or execution capability. Invalid, stale or unrecordable books are unavailable through the API. Snapshot depth is partial; never treat it as full-market liquidity. Retention eviction and crash tails limit historical coverage; use finalized, checksum-verified segments and state these limits in research.

M3 discovery and scanner results are preliminary research only. Fee scenarios, BNB discounts and promotional zero-maker assumptions are unverified, not account entitlements. Rankings allocate no capital and assert no executable edge. Gross spread capture is reduced by two-sided fees, observed proxy/assumed adverse selection, inventory allowance, rebalance costs and a safety margin. Negative/no-edge findings must be reported directly. Longer observations, realistic queue/fill simulation, independent risk and an approved controlled workflow are required before any future trading.

M4 local simulations use bounded cash/asset reservations, price/lot/notional checks, position caps, stale/disconnect invalidation, drawdown stops and an asset-balanced in-memory ledger. These are modeled controls, not a production risk system or durable OMS. Unknown coverage invalidates profitability and does not imply actual orders were canceled. Public MBP cannot establish exact queue rank. Hypothetical discounts/rebates are not verified account entitlements. A positive modeled result must remain positive after closeout costs and removal of favorable inventory gains; no positive result exists in the current short run. Do not advance to M5 without explicit approval.

M4.1 adds diagnostics and synthetic model validation only. Diagnostic reach and post-cancel counterfactuals are not fills. Exact-price trade-through omissions and estimated queue rank must be explicit; controlled synthetic fills are never profitability evidence. Public aggregate `m=true` means buyer maker / sell aggressor, and `m=false` means buy aggressor. Historical short-sample diagnosis does not authorize evaluating new OOS outcomes. No M5, live transport, leverage, credentials or capital allocation is enabled.

M4.2 funded initial assets are declared accounting holdings valued from a causal book; conversion executions/costs are not silently invented. Every row declares isolated capital or a validated partition of one $5,000 budget. Quote/base/BNB reservations include applicable configured fees and buffers; partial fills must leave other orders' reservations funded before posting any ledger changes. Unmodeled fee insufficiency invalidates execution rather than spending reserved assets. Through evidence remains modeled execution, not exact observable FIFO or actual own fills. Standard-fee research remains preliminary and no initial-inventory appreciation alone establishes edge. Preserve running captures; no private endpoint, real order, borrowing, leverage or M5 is enabled.


M5 explicitly authorizes public-data forward paper simulation only. Research profiles may use knowingly uneconomic widths but always pay the declared manual maker fees. Economic profiles require modeled round-trip fees plus 7bps for adverse selection, inventory and safety; this proxy does not validate executable edge. Both retain post-only activation, conservative through evidence, funding reservations, $100 clips, $500 inventory deviation limits, a $50 drawdown stop and stale/gap invalidation. Initial 50/50 assets are declared simulated holdings, not exchange conversions. MTM P&L includes initial inventory appreciation and excludes hypothetical liquidation costs; report its components separately.

Atomic local checkpoints preserve the simulated wallet and stop state. Virtual quotes do not remain executable across process downtime: restart voids them, labels unknown coverage, preserves balances and requires fresh books. These are paper lifecycle semantics, not reconciliation of actual exchange orders. Missing raw prefixes or crash tails prevent full-history reproduction. Do not use these smoke results to authorize live execution. Stop after M5.
