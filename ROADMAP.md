# Phased roadmap

1. **Foundation (completed):** Rust workspace and domain/exchange contracts; simulation/paper configuration; TypeScript health API; PostgreSQL schema; Compose; CI and unit/integration checks. No execution. Acceptance: build/typecheck/tests pass, migrations apply, unsupported modes fail closed.
2. **Public market data (implemented and validated):** Binance Spot public adapter, exact normalized decimals, snapshot/delta reconstruction, gap recovery, stale detection, immutable recording and Parquet. Acceptance: sequence-gap fixtures and reconnect replay pass without private credentials.
3. **Market scanner and profitability research (completed):** public symbol discovery, bounded multi-pair collection, horizon statistics, manual fee scenarios, conservative economic screening, deterministic research replay, reports and read-only API. Preliminary evidence only; no executable edge assumed.
4. **Execution backtesting (implemented; preliminary evidence):** deterministic trade-evidence queue simulation, latency/partial-fill/cancel races, fee-asset ledger, baseline strategy, stress scenarios, reports and bounded 24–72h capture. Existing short-sample experiments demonstrate no fills/edge. Long collection is ongoing, not completed validation. See docs/MILESTONE_4.md.
5. **Forward paper trading (implemented):** public BTCUSDT feeds, funded simulated execution, research/economic profiles, terminal monitoring, checkpoints and restart recovery. Functional smoke evidence only; sustained profitability validation remains outstanding. Next.js is deferred because the terminal meets this milestone. See MILESTONE_5.md.
7. **Future separately approved live pilot:** only after proven risk controls and credible net-expectancy evidence; fresh official API/filter review, LIMIT_MAKER behavior, scoped permissions and a strict small-capital budget. No leverage, borrowing or futures. Scale only after pilot evidence.

M1–M6 are approved. M6 is paper-only profitability discovery, not a live pilot. Stop after M6; any subsequent milestone/live execution requires fresh explicit approval. Each milestone ends with functionality, executed checks, limitations, safety review, documentation, commit and proposed next step. Multi-exchange coordination/hedging stays an extension point until justified.

**M4.1 (implemented):** auditable quote/intent/reach diagnostics, corrected multi-order FIFO, controlled synthetic execution tests, and streaming live-capture integrity/readiness audit. The original zero-fill history is unchanged; long-session profitability validation remains incomplete. M5 was subsequently approved and implemented; no live execution is authorized.

Longer profitability validation remains blocked by incomplete/gapped capture and modeling uncertainty; M5 was subsequently approved and implemented; no live execution is authorized.

See MILESTONE_6.md for actual findings and whether any candidate qualifies. No live pilot is enabled.
